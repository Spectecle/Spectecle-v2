# UX profile — spectecle.com

Read by the VectorLab UX skills (`.claude/skills/`) during audits.

## Aesthetic
Editorial, warm, restrained. Cream canvas, ink text, one copper accent (`#9a5423` / `#cb7c46`).
Serif display (Fraunces) for headings, Hanken Grotesk for UI and body.

## Type
- Weights: 300 (serif display), 400, 600. Nothing above 600.
- Text floor 12px. Body line-height 1.5–1.6; headings 1.1–1.25.
- Prose columns capped at 70ch.

## Accessibility
- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Hit targets: 44×44px. Standalone links get a 44px-tall hit area via `::after` in `globals.css`
  (visual size unchanged). Hero slide dots are 24×44.
- Focus: 2px copper ring on `:focus-visible`.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is honored site-wide (framer `MotionConfig`, CSS override, no Lenis, no hero autoplay).

## Voice
Plain, confident, short. Buttons are verb-first, sentence case ("Book a free call").
Navigation links and small eyebrow labels may be uppercase; buttons may not.

## Motion
App/UI chrome follows the 100 / 150 / 200 / 300ms token scale, transform/opacity only.

**Documented marketing exceptions** (hero and ambient brand motion only):
- Home hero headline reveal: 1050ms transform, once on load.
- Home hero slideshow: 1400ms opacity crossfade, 8s Ken Burns zoom, auto-advance every 5.6s
  (paused under reduced motion; arrows and dots always available).
- Service ticker marquee (30s loop) and testimonial marquee (70s loop): decorative, pause on hover,
  stopped under reduced motion.
- Lenis smooth scroll (1.4s easing): disabled under reduced motion.

## Banned
Default VectorLab ban list (rainbow CTAs, glassmorphism, cartoon people, decorative pills,
icons in inputs, shadow-only separation). Shadows stay below 0.08 alpha.
